# Code of conduct

Living System is about people staying in charge of their work and growing their skills. We want taking part to feel the same way: safe, respectful and useful for everyone, whatever their background, experience or technical level.

## What we expect

- Be kind, patient and constructive. Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Welcome beginners. There are no silly questions, and plain words are always fine.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Respect privacy: do not share anyone's personal information, and do not post private or confidential content.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution, and explain why when they do.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or ignoring a request to stop.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainer privately through the [Security tab's private reporting](https://github.com/the-trinity-tribe/living-system/security/advisories/new) (it reaches the maintainer only), and say it is a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may block people who do so repeatedly or seriously.

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.
