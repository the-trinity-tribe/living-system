## What counts as canonical

- this file;
- `PRINCIPLES.md`;
- files in `decisions/` with `status: approved` at the top.

Canonical means approved by a human and current. Everything else is working or background context.

## Authority

Approved repository state outranks agent inference. When information conflicts with canonical state, the conflict must be surfaced to a human, not quietly resolved. Agents must not invent organisational facts. The full authority order and procedures are in `AGENTS.md`.
