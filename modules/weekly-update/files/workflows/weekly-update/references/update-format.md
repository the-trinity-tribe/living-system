# Weekly update format

Stable reference for stage 2. Change it to fit your audience; the workflow will follow your version.

1. **Done this week:** at most 3 bullets, facts only.
2. **Next week:** at most 3 bullets.
3. **Needs a decision or help:** what you need from the reader, or "nothing".
4. **Sign-off:** the approver's name, set in stage 3.

Rules:

- At most 150 words.
- Plain words, no internal jargon.
- No prices, promises or dates unless they are in the facts list.
