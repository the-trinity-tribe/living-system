# Stage 1: set the starting point

## Inputs

| Source | File | Scope | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human | What they want to change and why | All | The idea |
| Card | `../references/experiment-card.md` | All | The template |

## Process

1. Copy the card to `output/<experiment-name>-card.md` and fill in the question, the change, the time and the measures with the human.
2. Record the starting value of each measure now, or write 'not measured'.
3. Agree what result would keep or revert the change.

## Outputs

`output/<experiment-name>-card.md`

## Checkpoint

The human says yes to start.
