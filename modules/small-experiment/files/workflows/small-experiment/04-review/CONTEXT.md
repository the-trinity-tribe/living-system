# Stage 4: review and decide

## Inputs

| Source | File | Scope | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Card and results | the `<experiment-name>` files of stages 1 and 3 | All | What was planned and seen |

## Process

1. Write what was seen in plain words, with the limits: how few times, no comparison group, what else changed.
2. Compare with the keep and revert lines. The human decides: keep, revise (a new version and another round) or revert.
3. If it should become a rule, draft a proposal; never approve it.

## Outputs

`output/<experiment-name>-review.md`

## Checkpoint

The human decides.
