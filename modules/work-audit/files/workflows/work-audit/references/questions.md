# Work audit: questions

## Map
- What are the main responsibilities? Who (which role) holds each?
- What work repeats every day, week or month? How long does it take?
- Where do decisions get made, and who makes them?

## Friction
- What do you explain again and again? To whom?
- Where do you wait for information or approval?
- Where does context get lost (handover, holidays, new people)?
- What do you do that nobody would miss?

## Sort (for each piece of work)
- **Drop:** would anyone miss it?
- **Keep human:** does it need judgement, trust or care?
- **Support:** could a checklist, template or AI draft make it easier while you stay in charge?
- **Automate:** is it stable, repeated (three times or more), safe and easy to undo?
