# Stage 1: frame the question

## Inputs

| Source | File | Scope | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human | Their question | All | What to find out |

## Process

1. Write the question in one sentence, and what it is for.
2. Agree what counts as evidence, how recent it must be, and what is out of scope.
3. Agree when it is good enough (for example three good sources).

## Outputs

`output/<date>-question.md`

## Checkpoint

The human confirms the question.
