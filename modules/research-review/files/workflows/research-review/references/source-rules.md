# Source rules

- Prefer primary sources (the study, the law, the official page) over articles about them.
- Every source gets: title, author or organisation, date, link or place, and type (study, official, news, opinion, own notes).
- Note when a source is older than the question needs, or has an interest in the answer.
- A claim needs a source and the place in it (page, section, paragraph).
- Two good sources that disagree are a finding, not a problem: report both.
