# Stage 1: write down the request

## Inputs

| Source | File | Scope | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human | The request (mail, call notes) | All | What came in |
| Questions | `../references/intake-questions.md` | First two | What to capture |

## Process

1. Write what the client asks for, in their words, under a client code.
2. Leave out contact details and anything personal that is not needed.

## Outputs

`output/<date>-<client-code>-request.md`

## Checkpoint

The human confirms it is what was asked.
