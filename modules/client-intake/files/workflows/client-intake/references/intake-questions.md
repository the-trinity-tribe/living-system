# Intake questions

- What does the client ask for, in their own words?
- What problem is behind it? What happens if nothing is done?
- By when, and why then?
- Who decides on their side (a role, not a name)?
- What do they already have or tried?
- What is out of scope for us, or a risk (for example data protection, a deadline we cannot meet)?
- What do we not know yet?
