# Second-check checklist

Stable reference for stage 2. Add lines for your own kind of work.

| # | Check | Pass when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Brief | The work does what the brief asked, and nothing the brief did not ask. |
| 2 | Facts | Every fact, number and date can be traced to the brief or a named file. Nothing is made up. |
| 3 | Commitments | The work promises nothing (prices, dates, scope) the brief or a decision does not allow. |
| 4 | Rules | Nothing conflicts with a current decision. Conflicts are named with the decision file. |
| 5 | Gaps | Anything missing is marked "not recorded", not filled in. |
| 6 | Reader | Someone who was not in the conversation can understand it. |
| 7 | Privacy | It contains nothing the workspace's privacy limits (`SYSTEM.md`) rule out. |
