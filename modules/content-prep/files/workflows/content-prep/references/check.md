# Content check

- Does it say what the brief asked, to the audience it named?
- Is every fact, number and quote backed by a source the human has? Unsupported claims are removed or marked.
- Does it promise anything the human cannot keep?
- Is the tone right for the channel and the audience?
- Plain words, short sentences, headings where they help; images have text alternatives.
- Names of people or clients only with their permission.
