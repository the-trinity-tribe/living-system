# Team onboarding note

Background, not canonical. Use this to bring a new team member into the workspace.

1. Give them the approved decisions that apply to them (run `python3 tools/review.py current` to list them).
2. Tell them that the agent proposes and people with approval rights decide.
3. Show them `proposals/` so they see how a change is suggested.
4. Ask them to read `SYSTEM.md` and say what is wrong or missing. Wrong or missing parts become proposals, not silent edits.
5. Agree where private notes live. Private notes stay outside this shared workspace.
